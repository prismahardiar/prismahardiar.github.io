Pemodelan Dinamis dan Kendali Optimal Produksi Energi Biodiesel Menggunakan Mikroalga dengan Strategi Kultivasi Dua Tahap

October 20, 2026 · Prismahardi Aji Riyantoko, Nur Ilmayasinta · Square
Download
  • Paper
Abstract
Microalgae biodiesel production must balance lipid accumulation against operating energy. This study develops a nonlinear five-state dynamic model of nonlipid biomass, lipids, nitrogen, inorganic carbon, and relative photosynthetic activity for two-stage cultivation, with a hybrid jump map for medium replacement. Six parameters are estimated: carbon fixation capacity ($p_{\max}$), light attenuation ($k_L$), $\mathrm{CO}_2$ response ($k_C$), specific nitrogen requirement ($q_N$), stress-induced photosynthetic decline ($k_d$), and basal lipid allocation ($\theta_0$). Calibration uses nine paired biomass and lipid observations of Chlorella sorokiniana on day 14. Mathematical analysis checks solution nonnegativity and mass balance; numerical evaluation applies cross-validation, bounded control, and switching-time comparisons. Calibration RMSEs are 0.721 and 0.311 g/L for biomass and lipids, increasing to 1.213 and 0.381 g/L under leave-one-treatment-out validation. Over 14 days in a 0.2 L culture, switching on day 12 maximizes partial biodiesel energy among candidate days 2–12 (2.076 kJ), but remains 7.20% below the reference batch (2.237 kJ). Medium replacement penalizes energy performance, while prediction across treatments remains uncertain. The contribution is a joint evaluation of medium-replacement state jumps, biomass retention, and switching-energy costs within partial biodiesel energy.
